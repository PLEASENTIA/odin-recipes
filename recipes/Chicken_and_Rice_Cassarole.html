Chicken and Rice Casserole

Description

This recipe is so easy to make. Just cover the casserole with foil and bake until the chicken is cooked through and the rice is tender. Yum!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of uncooked white rice
  • 4 boneless, skinless, chicken breast halves
  • 1 (10.5-ounce) can condensed cream of chicken soup
  • 1 7/8 cups water
  • 1 (1-ounce)package dry onion soup mix

Directions

    Step 1:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

    2. Step 2:

  2. Spread rice in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Arrange chicken peices on top of rice.

    3. Step 3:

  3. Whisk condensed soup and water together; pour over the chicken and rice. Sprinkle onion soup mix evenly over the top. Cover the dish tightly with foil.

    4. Step 4:

  4. Bake in the preheated oven until the rice is tender, the liquid is absorbed, and the chicken reaches 165 degrees F (74 degrees C), 1 to 1 1/2 hours. Begin checking after 1 hour. Serve hot.
